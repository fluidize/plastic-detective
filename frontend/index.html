      Welcome to Plastic Detective

      Scan a product's packaging, see what it's made of, find places to recycle it, and log the litter you find or pick up.

      1. Scan - Enter a 12-digit barcode. Optionally open Usage context (microwaved, reused, contents, notes) to improve the analysis. The app checks Open Food Facts, searches the web, and asks the LLM for the packaging material, polymers, additives, warnings, risk notes, and confidence.
      2. Recycling - Press Find recycling near me to use your location. The map shows nearby recycling points (OpenStreetMap), and the list gives distances plus Google Maps links.
      3. Litter Log - Click the map to fill select a coordinate, type in a litter type, and add it. You can edit or delete entries at any time.
      4. Settings - Change the SearXNG URL, number of search results, and the LLM model.

      Confidence is the model's self-reported certainty (0–100%) that the packaging details are correct — it is not a risk score.
